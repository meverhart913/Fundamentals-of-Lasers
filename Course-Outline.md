# Course Overview

This course is designed to take a student from **no formal optics background** to the level expected of a practicing laser engineer capable of:

- Reading laser engineering literature
- Understanding manufacturer datasheets
- Selecting laser materials
- Designing resonators
- Choosing pump sources
- Evaluating thermal performance
- Understanding crystal manufacturing
- Diagnosing laser failures
- Designing complete laser systems

The course emphasizes **engineering decision making**, not memorization.
